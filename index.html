Counterfactual discovery pathways

Sixteen examples across eight SciPaths comparison types. Each pair compares a recorded pathway for a scientific target with an edited pathway or a pathway from a related target.

Where the labels and rationales come from
Recorded pathway · label 1
The dataset’s reference pathway. Its necessity rationales and evidence passages are recorded SciPaths annotations.
Counterfactual variant · label 0
The negative example’s assigned dataset label. The analysis explains the edit. Example 2 also includes the saved GPT-5.6 assessment.
Neighbor swap and Neighbor recipe
The recorded pathway is preferred for the target in ranking training. The comparison pathway has no binary validity label.
Replacement component
Its recorded rationale comes from the source pathway in which it originally appeared. It explains its role there. The comparison analysis addresses whether it serves the new target.
